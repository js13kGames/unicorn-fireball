Run around a plain full of unicorns, collect the ones that match your colour, then turn the whole herd into a rainbow fireball and ram it into somebody.

How it works:

- 77 unicorns, 7 colours, 7 leaders (you plus 6 AI).
-  Unicorns of your colour join you when you pass them. Your followers recruit too.
-  Hold Space to charge. Full charge = your herd becomes a fireball you ride. Bigger herd, longer charge, bigger fireball.
-  Fireball vs fireball: bigger herd wins. Loser's herd scatters and goes wild, free for anyone to collect. Loser drops a heart.
-  3 hearts. Only fireballs take hearts. At 0 you're a golden statue and your colour goes wild too.
-  Horn-to-horn bumps knock a few unicorns loose from both herds but don't cost hearts.
-  Running off the edge of the plain kills you.

It's a Kamehameha charge-up where you have to go round up the energy first, and losing the beam struggle means the other guy gets to keep your energy.

Arrow keys or WASD to steer, up to sprint, down to slow, Space to charge. Press O on the title screen to join the online plain. Under 13 KB, everything generated at load, plays on mobile.
